{% comment %} Closing call to action. One per page, always the last stratum. This is the canonical block: the homepage uses it too, so the two cannot drift apart. Defaults suit every page written so far; override only if a page genuinely needs a different question: {% include stratum-cta.html lede="Your question." %} Single column, deliberately. This block ran on the .duo grid with three paragraphs of body copy in the right-hand column. Only the first of them said anything — that the call is free — and the other two were there to stop the column looking empty. There is no body copy now and there should not be one, because nothing that could go here is true for every reader. Someone on their fifth ISO 27001 cycle, someone holding a customer questionnaire they can already read, and someone who has just lost a tender arrive at completely different points. Any sentence promising what the call produces is either false for two of them or too vague to mean anything, and a caveat about fit belongs on /services/ where a reader can act on it, not directly above the button. The weight the paragraphs were carrying comes from type size instead: see .lede--lg in css/typography.css, which exists for this block alone. The empty space to the right of the question is the end of the page. Resist putting a second column back into it. There is no `body` parameter any more. If a page ever needs one, that is a sign the page needs a different closing block, not that this one needs a paragraph. {% endcomment %}
