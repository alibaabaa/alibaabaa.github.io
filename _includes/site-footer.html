{%- comment %} Statutory trading disclosure. Companies Act 2006 s.82 with the Names and Trading Disclosures Regulations 2015 requires the registered name on the site (reg 24(2)) and, with it, the part of the UK of registration and the registered number (reg 25). Its own row below the meta line rather than a fourth cell inside it: the meta row is a two-cell justified layout and a third item wraps into a ragged stack on a narrow viewport. A separate rail also gives the office address and VAT number somewhere to go later without a layout decision. Every particular is read from site.company, so the values live in _config.yml and appear on the site in exactly one place. The last two are conditional: unset in config, they render nothing at all rather than an empty separator. Each particular is its own span so the line breaks between items rather than through the company number. The separators are aria-hidden; the whitespace around them is what a screen reader gets, which is why they sit on their own lines here and must stay that way. {% endcomment -%}
